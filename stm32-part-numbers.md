# STM32 alphabet soup, aka what's in a series letter

<qyx> Wat? "F" means "Foundation line" ?
<peterm> Yeah, I'm pretty sure they were the generic/baseline parts,

The L parts were F parts but lower power, H parts were F parts but higher performance,
C parts were F parts but cheaper/shitter, U parts were the F parts but "ultra" lower power,
W parts were F parts but wireless, N parts were F parts but Nobody cared
